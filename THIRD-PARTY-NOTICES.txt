Laminar Dock (the AuraExplorer panel)
================================
The panel — the dock strip, its tray, stacks, previews, search and the Launchpad
frame in crates/aura-dock and crates/laminar-sys — is vendored from Laminar Dock
0.2.9 by Dave Sousa, commit bcf3f33 (https://github.com/davesousa/Laminar_Dock).
AuraExplorer changes it: the Launchpad keeps one panel instead of three, the panel is
started by AuraExplorer.exe instead of a separate shortcut, Velopack is replaced with
AuraExplorer's own upgrade path, and the settings file lives in %APPDATA%\AuraExplorer\Dock.
The upstream source declares @license MIT. The full licence text is in
packaging\licenses\Laminar-MIT.txt.
